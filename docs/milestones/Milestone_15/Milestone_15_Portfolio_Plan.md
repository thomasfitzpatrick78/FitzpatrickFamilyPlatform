## Purpose

This document publishes the approved Milestone 15 portfolio direction.

Milestone 15 increases Engineering Organization throughput through delivery leverage. It reuses the governance, contracts, validators, evidence models, and architecture published in earlier milestones before considering expansion.

This plan records the portfolio direction, the separately published EO-15.1 and PLAT-15.1A work packages, the exact accepted EO-14.2A delayed-effect authorization subject, and the serialized delayed-effect publication target for the exact unassigned-candidate Goal-Oriented Parallel Delivery passive-capability work package. Authority remains in each exact published work package or subject; this plan does not by itself assign an initiative or authorize implementation, FFFA implementation, live infrastructure work, governance expansion, role activation, automation activation, deployment, observation, or release.

EO-15.1 repository implementation is Architecture Gatekeeper approved and published. This planning update records the approved publication state only and makes no new portfolio decision.

The exact EO-14.2A synthetic-fixture consumer authorization subject, SHA-256 `12d639bf5251ad27c2cd6e1b79dcc8d696ce7cab89d20854b2d991a91319871e`, is accepted for publication with delayed effect. Charlie implementation remains unstarted throughout this publication package. Authority for a separately initialized repository-only implementation becomes effective only after the exact subject publication is committed, pushed, and local, tracking, and live remote equality are proved. Final provider/live, named-target, trend, dashboard, metrics, activation, implementation-publication, production, and customer gates remain closed.

The exact Goal-Oriented Parallel Delivery passive-capability work package, SHA-256 `dde123b3fff5aff9fe432082f1337e9b6aeae486b02f460db374bd975341faf3`, is accepted for serialized publication-package preparation after Charlie with delayed effect. The exact file's outside/not-published/next-acceptance language is immutable preparation-state provenance required by its accepted digest; this plan, the roadmap, backlog, Kanban, and continuity records are the authoritative current publication-lifecycle statements after application. `CANDIDATE-EO-15.2` remains unassigned. Publication of the exact prepared bytes does not authorize implementation; a later initiative assignment and status amendment, fetched and synchronized initialization, Architecture Integration shared-path lease, implementation acceptance, implementation publication, pilot, activation, production, release, and live-work decision remain separate.

PLAT-15.1A is Product Strategy Board selected and Architecture Gatekeeper approved and published as the High, highest-priority Platform implementation package. The Board and Gatekeeper have additionally approved the version 1.0 supported-Linux environment-preparation architecture under existing PLAT-15.1A / PLAT-PB-013 and AB-011 authority: UTM/Apple Virtualization, strict offline isolation, bounded acquisition/read-only sealing, Canonical snapshot `20260725T000000Z`, host-worktree implementation, guest-only `platrun` validation, mandatory media detachment, distinct evidence exports, protected-local host data, and the 17-gate lifecycle. Exact Go 1.26.5 validation passed with retrieval disabled. The preparation work package remains unstaged and unpublished pending separate publication authorization. The environment is `Not Created`, repository implementation is `Not Started`, and PLAT-15.1A is `Blocked at Supported-Linux Initialization Gate`.

The earlier direction to "Deliver additional Platform implementation" is now resolved specifically to PLAT-15.1A; it is not a continuing generic or unassigned Platform outcome.

The approved portfolio direction remains: Operationalize the approved Transition Review through EO-15.1 while reusing existing lifecycle and evidence mechanisms.

## Theme

Increase Engineering Organization Throughput through Delivery Leverage.

Delivery leverage means:

- reuse existing governance before expanding it;
- reuse proven governance and contracts;
- shorten repeated orientation and reconciliation work;
- preserve evidence and approval boundaries;
- focus implementation effort on approved customer and Platform outcomes;
- promote practices only after repeated evidence.

## Delivery-Leverage Principles

1. Reuse EO-14.1A execution contracts and EO-14.4A orchestration boundaries where applicable.
2. Reuse AI Collaboration Governance for initialization, continuity, completion, and readiness.
3. Reuse Platform Operations evidence, reconciliation, health, confidence, and provider/consumer boundaries.
4. Reuse existing validation and report mechanisms before creating new ones.
5. Preserve current-state versus planned-state distinctions.
6. Do not promote Capability-First Operationalization, AB-012, or another candidate practice without repeated approved evidence.
7. Do not treat customer acceptance, repository implementation, architecture approval, activation, or live work as interchangeable.
8. Treat repository-generated engineering evidence as a distinct fail-closed baseline class only through the published policy, attribution, validation, and exact work-package permission gates.

## Evidence Expectations

Milestone 15 evidence should show:

- completed FFFA customer acceptance or an explicit evidence-based stop decision;
- PLAT-15.1A repository implementation completed only within its exact published package;
- published EO-15.1 implementation and validation applied only through repository-authorized future sessions;
- exact EO-14.2A subject publication and remote-equality proof before any Charlie implementation, followed by evidence that implementation remains within its fixture-only, pass-through, recommendation-only boundary;
- exact Goal-Oriented Parallel Delivery work-package publication after Charlie with no initiative assignment, implementation, runtime dispatch, persistence, scheduling, model invocation, pilot, or activation;
- recurring generated-evidence baseline approval removed through a fail-closed repository classification that rejects all additional, ambiguous, or unauthorized changes;
- measurable delivery leverage from reuse without unsupported throughput precision;
- continued repository, governance, AI readiness, and engineering-test health;
- no governance promotion without repeated evidence.
